# Learn Mandarin · 轻松学中文

Easy Mandarin Chinese for English speakers: pinyin, tones, characters and real conversations.
One set of content powers two outputs: an interactive web app and a printable book.

**Live site: https://learnmandarin.zyeemzia.com/**

## Open it

| What | How |
|---|---|
| Web app | https://learnmandarin.zyeemzia.com/ (installs an offline cache on first visit) |
| Printable book | Open `book.html` → Ctrl + P → Save as PDF / print (A4, background graphics on) |
| Ready PDF | `Learn-Mandarin-Book.pdf` |

## What's inside

6 units · 60 lessons · 402 quiz questions · 389 vocabulary words · 112 word-by-word sentences · 31 conversations

1. **Pinyin & Tones**: The sounds of Mandarin: initials, finals, the four tones and how to read pinyin
2. **Characters & Writing**: Strokes, stroke order, radicals and your first 17 characters
3. **First Words & Phrases**: Greetings, numbers, money, time, family, food and getting around
4. **Grammar Made Easy**: Build real sentences step by step: word order, questions, negatives and time
5. **Everyday Conversations**: Real-life Mandarin for restaurants, shops, travel, phones, plans, the doctor and work.
6. **Reading & Culture**: Graded stories, a Tang poem, chengyu, festivals and customs.

**Web app extras:** built-in natural Mandarin voices (female and male) for every word and sentence, so pronunciation works on every device with nothing to install,
a writing studio (real stroke-order animations and stroke-by-stroke writing quizzes, dictation),
daily missions with XP and streaks, word-by-word sentences with a memorise mode, conversations with play-all and role-play,
Letter Hunt, Listen & Pick, Word Match, Sentence Builder and Speed Quiz games, radicals,
spaced-repetition flashcards, a downloadable certificate, a sound setup guide for every device,
light and dark theme and adjustable Mandarin text size.

**Book extras:** every lesson with copybook writing pages, tian-zi-ge character grids and stroke-by-stroke diagrams,
word-search and sentence puzzles, radicals, a journal, answer keys, a progress tracker and a certificate.

Fonts are bundled in `fonts/` under the SIL Open Font License. Stroke data in `hanzi/` comes from the open Hanzi Writer data set (Make Me a Hanzi, Arphic Public License).
